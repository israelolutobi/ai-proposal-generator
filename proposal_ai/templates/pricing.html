When does my allowance reset?

Both plans reset at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each calendar month. Unused proposals do not roll over. Your Pro payment renews monthly from the date you subscribe; its billing date can differ from your allowance reset.

What counts as a proposal?

Each successfully saved AI proposal, including a regeneration, counts once. A request in progress temporarily reserves one place. Failed requests and replaying the same completed request do not use another monthly place. Profile summaries and job extraction do not use proposal places. Fair-use burst limits and service spending controls also apply.

What happens when I upgrade or cancel?

Upgrading raises your current month's allowance to 50, including proposals already generated. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Pro stays available until the paid period ends. You then return to Free with the same month's usage. Your saved proposals, profile and experience stay available.

What if a payment fails?

Pro access pauses while payment needs attention. You can keep using your account with Free limits and update your payment method in Manage billing. Pro returns when Stripe confirms an active, paid subscription.
