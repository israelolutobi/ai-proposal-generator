ProposalQ uses artificial intelligence to help freelancers understand job opportunities, prepare professional profiles and generate tailored proposal drafts. This document explains how these features operate, their limitations and the responsibilities of users.

1. How ProposalQ Uses AI

ProposalQ provides AI-assisted functionality including:

  • Generating freelancer profile-summary drafts.
  • Extracting structured information from freelance job descriptions.
  • Generating proposals based on freelancer experience and job requirements.

These features are intended to assist users with their work. They do not replace professional judgement or guarantee successful applications.

2. Information Processed by AI

Depending on the feature selected, information transmitted to the AI provider may include:

  • Professional titles and skills supplied by users.
  • Freelancer profile information and work experience.
  • Job descriptions and associated client information.
  • Confirmed job requirements and application context.
  • Relevant research-derived context used to inform proposal generation.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary confidential information, personal identifiers or information they are not authorised to share.

3. Third-Party AI Processing

ProposalQ's current AI integration uses the OpenAI API to process submitted information and produce generated responses.

Information included in an AI request is transmitted to the provider for processing. Applicable data-handling arrangements, retention and privacy protections are subject to the provider's terms and ProposalQ's configuration.

4. Accuracy and Reliability

AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies, omissions, misinterpretations or unsuitable recommendations.

ProposalQ includes instructions and validation mechanisms intended to reduce unsupported claims and improve output relevance. However, these mechanisms cannot guarantee that every response will be accurate or appropriate.

5. Human Review and Responsibility

Users should review and edit AI-generated material before submitting it to clients or publishing it on freelance platforms.

Users remain responsible for checking factual claims, qualifications, experience, proposed deliverables and compliance with the rules of any third-party platform they use.

6. Research-Assisted Generation

ProposalQ may incorporate research-derived context when generating proposals. This information is intended to help identify relevant requirements and improve the structure of generated drafts.

Research observations do not establish that a particular proposal will succeed or that an individual user will achieve a specific result.

7. No Guaranteed Outcomes

ProposalQ does not guarantee interviews, client responses, contract awards, earnings or other commercial outcomes.

Freelance application results depend on factors outside ProposalQ's control, including client decisions, competition, pricing, qualifications and market conditions.

8. AI Usage Information

ProposalQ records operational information relating to AI requests, including request status, model identifiers, token usage, timestamps and certain processing outcomes.

Further information about personal data processing will be provided in the Privacy Policy.

9. Policy Status

This document is a draft based on the application's reviewed implementation. It remains subject to operational verification, business approval and legal review before publication.
