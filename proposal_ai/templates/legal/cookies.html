This Cookie Policy explains how ProposalQ uses cookies and similar technologies to support its website and application.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. They can help websites maintain login sessions, remember preferences and support security functionality.

2. Essential Cookies

ProposalQ is built using Django, which supports cookies for authentication sessions and protection against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).

The following cookies are associated with Django's standard functionality. Their actual use and configuration must be confirmed against the deployed application.

Cookie Purpose Typical duration
sessionid Maintains an authenticated user session. Up to 2 weeks by Django default, subject to configuration.
csrftoken Supports protection against forged requests. Up to 1 year by Django default, subject to configuration.

3. Cookie Security

ProposalQ's reviewed Django configuration enables the Secure attribute for session and CSRF cookies in production.

This attribute instructs compatible browsers to transmit those cookies only over secure HTTPS connections.

4. Analytics and Optional Cookies

The presence, purposes and providers of any analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies have not yet been fully verified.

Before any non-essential cookies are deployed, ProposalQ must assess applicable consent requirements and implement appropriate controls where required.

5. Third-Party Services

ProposalQ integrates with external providers, including Stripe for billing and OpenAI for AI-assisted processing.

Some external services may operate on their own websites or hosted interfaces and apply their own cookie policies.

Users should consult the relevant provider's privacy and cookie information when using those external services.

6. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow users to inspect, block or delete cookies through their settings.

Blocking essential cookies may prevent authentication or other application functions from working correctly.

7. Updates to This Policy

This document may require updates when ProposalQ introduces new integrations, analytics services or cookie functionality.
