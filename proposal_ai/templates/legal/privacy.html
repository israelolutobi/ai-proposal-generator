ProposalQ is an AI-assisted platform designed to help freelancers analyse job opportunities, prepare proposals and track applications. This Privacy Policy explains the categories of personal information the platform processes and the purposes for which it is used.

1. Who Is Responsible for Your Information?

ProposalQ is responsible for determining how personal information is processed when operating its platform, to the extent that it acts as a data controller under applicable data-protection law.

The legal identity, business contact details and privacy contact information of the responsible operator must be confirmed before this policy is published.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how the service is used, ProposalQ may process:

  • Account information associated with registration and authentication.
  • Professional titles, skills and freelancer profile summaries.
  • Work experience, professional responsibilities and project histories.
  • Freelance job descriptions, requirements, budgets and client information.
  • AI-generated proposals, application records and recorded outcomes.
  • AI request metadata, including usage counts, model identifiers and processing status.
  • Subscription records, Stripe customer identifiers and billing status.
  • Information submitted through any available support or contact channels.

3. How Information Is Used

ProposalQ processes information to:

  • Provide and administer user accounts.
  • Generate AI-assisted professional summaries and proposals.
  • Extract relevant information from freelance opportunities.
  • Save and organise user-created application records.
  • Administer subscription access and monthly usage allowances.
  • Protect the platform against misuse and operational failures.
  • Maintain service functionality and investigate technical issues.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

When users request AI-assisted features, relevant information is transmitted to OpenAI through its API.

Depending on the feature, this may include professional titles, skills, work experience, job descriptions, confirmed job details and research-derived application context.

Users should avoid submitting confidential information or personal data that is unnecessary for the requested service.

Additional information is available in the AI Transparency document.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

ProposalQ's reviewed architecture includes integrations with third-party service providers, including:

  • OpenAI for AI-assisted content processing.
  • Stripe for subscription billing and payment management.
  • Neon PostgreSQL for application database services.
  • Render for application hosting and deployment.

The specific contractual arrangements, processing locations, international transfer safeguards and applicable retention periods require verification before publication.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

ProposalQ maintains subscription information including Stripe customer identifiers, subscription identifiers, payment status, billing periods and cancellation status.

Payment collection and customer billing management are integrated with Stripe. The precise allocation of payment-data responsibilities must be confirmed against the live payment configuration.

7. Cookies and Sessions

ProposalQ uses Django-based account and request-security functionality, which may involve session and CSRF cookies.

Details of the cookies used by the deployed service, including their purposes and durations, will be provided in the Cookie Policy following verification.

8. Data Storage and Security

ProposalQ uses database-backed storage for application records and implements security-related controls within its Django configuration and AI request-handling workflows.

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Further information is available in the Data & Security document.

9. Legal Bases for Processing

Under UK data-protection law, personal information must be processed on an appropriate lawful basis.

The applicable lawful bases for each processing activity, including account administration, AI processing, billing, security monitoring and any optional analytics, must be documented and confirmed before publication.

10. International Data Transfers

Some third-party services may process information outside the United Kingdom.

Relevant processing locations and any required international data-transfer safeguards must be verified before this policy is finalised.

11. Data Retention

ProposalQ stores account-related, professional, application, AI usage and subscription records.

Specific retention periods, deletion procedures and any legal obligations requiring continued retention must be confirmed before publication.

12. Your Data-Protection Rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, individuals may have rights to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request erasure of personal information.
  • Request restriction of certain processing.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the lawful basis.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

A verified privacy contact and request-handling process must be established before this policy is published.

13. Complaints

Individuals in the United Kingdom may have the right to raise data-protection concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Further information is available at ico.org.uk.

14. Changes to This Policy

ProposalQ may update its Privacy Policy to reflect changes in its services, technology, legal requirements or processing practices. An effective date and appropriate notification arrangements must be established before publication.
