This document explains the subscription structure currently implemented in ProposalQ, including monthly proposal allowances, payment arrangements and subscription cancellation.

1. Available Plans

ProposalQ's reviewed subscription configuration includes:

  • Free: 3 successful AI-generated proposals per calendar month.
  • Pro: 50 successful AI-generated proposals per calendar month.

The Pro subscription is configured at £9.99 per month, with tax-inclusive pricing.

Plan availability and purchasing functionality depend on whether ProposalQ's billing system is enabled.

2. Monthly Proposal Allowances

Proposal allowances are calculated using calendar months in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC).

The allowance resets at 00:00 UTC on the first day of each calendar month.

This reset schedule is separate from an individual customer's subscription billing anniversary.

Successful proposal-generation requests count towards the monthly allowance. Requests still being processed may temporarily reserve allowance capacity.

3. Subscription Payments

ProposalQ integrates with Stripe to administer subscription checkout, billing records and customer payment management.

When paid subscriptions are available, the applicable price and payment details should be presented during checkout.

The final commercial terms governing automatic renewal, payment failures and billing notifications require confirmation before publication.

4. Managing Your Subscription

ProposalQ includes an integration with the Stripe Customer Portal.

The configured portal supports subscription cancellation, payment-method updates and invoice history.

Access to these functions depends on the billing system being enabled and the customer's billing account being available.

5. Cancelling a Pro Subscription

ProposalQ's reviewed billing configuration requires subscription cancellation to be scheduled for the end of the current billing period.

Where a cancellation is successfully scheduled, Pro access may continue until the recorded paid subscription period ends, provided the subscription continues to satisfy ProposalQ's eligibility checks.

Cancellation does not automatically delete a ProposalQ user account or its stored information.

6. Expiry of Pro Access

Pro access depends on the subscription being active, paid and within its recorded subscription period.

When these conditions are no longer satisfied, ProposalQ applies the Free plan's proposal allowance.

7. Refunds and Statutory Rights

The commercial refund policy has not yet been finalised.

Any final refund terms must comply with applicable consumer-protection legislation and must not exclude rights that cannot legally be excluded.

The circumstances in which refunds may be requested, the applicable process and the relevant contact information must be confirmed before publication.

8. Service Changes

Subscription prices, allowances and available features may be revised in future.

Appropriate notice, contractual protections and customer choices must be established before implementing changes affecting existing subscriptions.

9. Billing Support

A verified customer-support contact and billing dispute procedure must be established before paid subscriptions are publicly launched.

10. Policy Status
